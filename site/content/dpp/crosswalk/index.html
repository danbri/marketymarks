dpp crosswalk (experimental)

marketymarks / dpp

Each field of the EU battery passport (EC 1.0.0), and what it is called in three other models: the Catena-X battery pass, the Battery Pass consortium's data model, and the UN Transparency Protocol's product passport. Each match is marked exact, close, broader, narrower or partial, with the main difference.

Every mapping was checked against the published source files of each model, at the versions linked above. Property names belong to their models; this table is a reading aid, not an official alignment. Not an official EC tool.

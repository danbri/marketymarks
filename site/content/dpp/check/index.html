marketymarks / dpp

Check an EU Digital Product Passport (EC JSON) against the European Commission's own published rules, release 1.0.0 (SHACL shapes and SKOS codelists). Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Covers batteries (EV, industrial, light means of transport) and the shared core.

dpp viewer (experimental)

marketymarks / dpp

Browse, search and compare battery passports. Field names and codes come from the EC's own vocabularies. The 8 demo passports are fictional; the Global Battery Alliance pilots are real, as published (notes).

Search runs in your browser. SPARQL loads a query engine (about 31 MB, once). Maps use OpenStreetMap; place names are looked up with OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Not an official EC tool.

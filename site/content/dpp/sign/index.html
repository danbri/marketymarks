dpp signing (experimental)

marketymarks / dpp

Sign a passport so anyone can check it hasn't been changed and who issued it. The EC JSON goes inside a W3C Verifiable Credential, signed with Data Integrity eddsa-rdfc-2022 (RDF canonicalization, then Ed25519), with a did:key issuer. Keys made here stay in this page.

A demonstration of signed passports, not an EC requirement: the EC Registry validates the plain JSON. A did:key proves only that the holder of that key signed; tying a key to a company needs a trusted identifier (for example did:web or an eIDAS seal). Not an official EC tool.
